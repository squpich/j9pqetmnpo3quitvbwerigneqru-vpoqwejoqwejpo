FAQ

Common questions before booking.

Are you a property management company?

No. We provide independent quality control and do not manage bookings, guests or daily operations.

Are you a cleaning company?

No. We verify cleaning quality as part of the wider guest experience.

Do I need to be present?

No. Access can be coordinated through the owner, cleaner, manager or self check-in system.

How long does an audit take?

It depends on the property size and scope. We confirm the expected duration before booking.

Can you fix problems?

Minor safe fixes can be completed during the visit. Larger work is quoted and scheduled separately.

Can you check my property manager's work?

Yes. Independent verification of management quality is one of the main use cases for the service.
